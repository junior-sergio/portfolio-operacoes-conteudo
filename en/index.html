Case 01

Writing QA & Editorial Governance System

Challenge

The content creation process relied heavily on manual reviews and consultations across multiple support documents, making the writing stage more time-consuming and prone to inconsistencies.

Solution

I designed a validation system directly within the content production tool, using automated Correction, Attention, and Standardization messages based on predefined editorial rules.
❌ Alert: Email subject line ending with a period automatically detected
❌ Alert: Card title exceeded the 30-character limit

Results

  • Greater editorial consistency across content pieces
  • Reduced rework during later production stages
  • Improved efficiency in the approval process
  • Centralized editorial knowledge within a single reference structure
Case 02

Content Automation at Scale

Context

A CRM operation produced segmented campaigns for different audiences, regions, and Beauty Consultant profiles. Each campaign could generate dozens of variations.

Challenge

A significant portion of the information was identical across campaign variations but still needed to be manually entered into every record. This created a high manual burden and increased error risk.

Solution

I redesigned the Airtable operational architecture by creating an intermediary layer to centralize content shared across all campaigns and use lookup fields for automatic inheritance.
Pre-Personalization Table: Shared fields registered once — Constraints, Template, and Product Type automatically distributed.
Main Personalization Table: Previously manual fields automatically inherited through Lookup fields — Campaign, Type, Brand, Constraints, Template, and more.

Impact

  • Significant reduction in manual work
  • Lower incidence of operational errors
  • Faster campaign creation and review cycles
  • More scalable structure for high-volume operations
  • Centralized maintenance of content and business rules
Case 03

Scalable Content Architecture — Template Library

Challenge

As content volume increased, challenges emerged around standardization, knowledge reuse, and production speed. Information and reference materials were scattered across multiple platforms.

Solution

I developed a modular knowledge system organized into three levels: Content Atoms (reusable components), Content Blocks (combinations of atoms), and Full Templates (complete, ready-to-use structures).
Module Library Overview: Category-based navigation, template filtering, and structured Content and Notes columns.
Content Column: Replicable content framework including text structure, variations, and dynamic fields.
Notes Column: Guidance for editors on how to adapt, personalize, and apply templates to different contexts.

Results

  • Faster content production with standardized building blocks
  • Improved consistency across all content variations
  • New team members onboard faster with clear templates
  • Knowledge centralized and easy to maintain and update
  • Higher-quality output through proven structures and guidelines
