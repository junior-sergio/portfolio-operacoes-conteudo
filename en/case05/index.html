CASE 05

Career Self-Awareness

Career OS started as a way to accelerate job applications. In the process, it became something else: a system for organizing experience, testing professional hypotheses, and gaining a clearer view of where my capabilities create value.

01

From better applications to a clearer career

The starting point was Light Applications (Case 04): systematizing the creation of better resumes, faster, to increase visibility and access to good opportunities.

As I structured profile, experience, skills, opportunities, and evidence, a discovery emerged that was not part of the original plan: the problem was not only producing better applications. It was understanding my own professional positioning more clearly.

How can professional experience become knowledge about my own career?

Career self-awareness here does not mean isolated introspection. It means building a clearer view of my trajectory through evidence, patterns, experiments, and results.

03

Career OS

The system evolved from an application automation workflow into a personal professional knowledge infrastructure.

EXPERIENCE → KNOWLEDGE → WORKFLOWS → OUTPUTS → LEARNING → REUSABLE KNOWLEDGE

The first official workflow, Career Discovery, organizes opportunities, professional profile, companies, research, context, and application materials.

04

What the system revealed

Professional repositioning

Structuring my experience revealed capabilities and professional patterns that had been scattered across my trajectory.

Systems Thinking

The focus expanded beyond content production to include process, structure, automation, and operational design.

AI as a capability

AI became part of workflows with context, criteria, validation, and learning — not simply a content generator.

Reusable knowledge

Relevant experiences can feed new decisions, workflows, and assets instead of disappearing when an application ends.

05

A result I was not looking for

After going through this repositioning process, I was spontaneously invited to an international selection process that was much more aligned with the positioning the system had helped me uncover.

The process progressed through selection and contracting, with a start date confirmed for September 2026.

LIGHT APPLICATIONS → REPOSITIONING → NEW POSITIONING → INTERNATIONAL OPPORTUNITY → SELECTION

The point is not to assign automatic causality between the system and the hiring outcome. The more important result was discovering and articulating a professional positioning that I had not previously formulated in this way.

06

What this project represents

Career self-awareness as a system.

Career OS is my laboratory for turning experience into evidence, evidence into knowledge, and knowledge into more conscious professional decisions.

  • Content Operations
  • Systems Thinking
  • AI Workflow Design
  • Knowledge Management
  • Cross-functional Translation

More than demonstrating tools, the project demonstrates a way of working: observe, structure, experiment, validate, learn, and evolve.
